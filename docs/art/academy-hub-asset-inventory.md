# Academy Hub Asset Inventory

**Status:** Exploratory production inventory — not a locked campus layout or art
brief
**Type:** Art, animation, audio, and content-facing asset planning
**Started:** 2026-08-24

## Purpose

List the production assets likely needed for the bounded, top-down Academy hub
to feel like a lively, used magical school and small campus city. This inventory
distinguishes foundational assets from variety and polish so that "complete"
does not quietly expand into an open-world art burden.

The working model behind this list is:

- one shared Practice Ground supports practice-themed battles;
- elemental biomes are field destinations reached through travel rather than
  four campus districts;
- the Campus Shop and competitive Arena remain physical campus anchors;
- teachers own major instructional arcs, while students, staff, the
  Merriweathers, visitors, discoveries, and later biome inhabitants broaden the
  quest-source roster;
- large lecture-hall interiors and four bespoke elemental teaching buildings
  are not assumed.

These are current working ideas, not additions to accepted world definition.

## What Exists Today

The current playable hub provides scale and interaction scaffolding, but almost
all of its world art is placeholder material:

- a tiled grass plane with island-edge cliff and surrounding water treatments;
- three placeholder building cutouts for the Shop, inherited Mission Hall, and
  competitive Arena;
- one generic student sprite, one generic guard sprite, and sheep used to judge
  crowd density;
- placeholder trees, bushes, reeds, water rocks, and rubber ducks;
- one placeholder player idle sheet and one placeholder player run sheet;
- five generic professor placements, including an inherited general professor;
- one generic Practice Grounds world target;
- basic battle music and a very small general sound-effects library.

None of those placeholders establishes final architecture, terrain, NPC design,
population, or campus geography. In particular, the inherited Mission Hall and
five-professor arrangement are not production-asset requirements.

## Completion Target

The hub should be considered visually content-ready when a player can:

1. identify its major functions from the world art rather than placeholder
   labels;
2. walk a coherent route through distinct but visually related campus spaces;
3. see several simultaneous signs of student, staff, commercial, and training
   activity;
4. find recurring characters at locations that make sense for what they are
   doing;
5. read the campus as a mystical place in the sky from within the playable
   view;
6. leave for an excursion from a visually legible departure point;
7. revisit the hub without every character and prop appearing frozen in the
   same pose.

## Tier 1 — Essential World Kit

These assets establish the campus and should exist before commissioning large
amounts of character or decorative variety.

### Campus Terrain

- primary walkable ground set with center, edge, corner, and transition pieces;
- island rim and exposed underside/cliff pieces that read clearly from the
  gameplay camera;
- path set with straight, corner, junction, end, and worn-edge variations;
- stairs, short ramps, low terraces, retaining edges, and small elevation
  transitions;
- safe-edge treatment where players cannot jump, such as planted borders,
  parapets, railings, or architectural walls;
- launch-edge treatment where jumping or flight is intentionally invited;
- small ground-detail overlays: seams, wear, patches, fallen leaves, petals,
  and repaired sections.

### Sky and Cloud Set

- stylized mystical cloud masses based on the selected National Palace Museum
  cloud-language reference;
- several cloud silhouettes and scales to prevent visible repetition;
- cloud banks that conceal the world boundary and lower portions of the island;
- cloud wisps or streamers that pass around architecture and cliff edges;
- slow ambient motion for at least one near layer and one distant layer;
- distant sky or horizon treatment that does not resemble ordinary ground-level
  water;
- island shadow or atmospheric-depth treatment where the camera can see below
  the campus.

### Shared Academy Architecture Kit

A reusable exterior kit should create most campus buildings without requiring a
unique painting for every structure:

- wall sections, corners, bases, and upper-story sections;
- roof sections, roof ends, ridges, and small towers or roof lanterns;
- doors, large entrances, windows, shutters, and balconies;
- arches, colonnades, exterior stairs, canopies, and covered walks;
- columns, railings, low walls, gates, and bridge pieces;
- campus signs, plaques, crests, and hanging identifiers;
- magical and mundane light fixtures;
- two or three material or color variations that still read as one institution;
- damaged, older, or repaired variants for limited historical texture.

### Core Landmark Exteriors

- **Central dungeon portal:** dark spherical portal treatment, containment or
  threshold architecture, and surrounding danger cues once its physical design
  is settled;
- **Campus Shop:** distinctive exterior, sign, entrance, awning or display
  treatment, delivery area, and small commercial prop set;
- **Practice Ground:** shared training surface and its supporting equipment;
- **Competitive Arena:** readable exterior or campus entrance appropriate to
  its existing feature route;
- **Expedition departure point:** overlook, flight terrace, dock, platform, or
  another final form chosen once travel fiction is settled;
- **two to four supporting façades or small structures** that imply residences,
  offices, dining, storage, or student life without requiring interiors.

### Practice Ground Kit

- main practice surface or summoning ring;
- boundary treatment and entrance;
- movable targets or training dummies;
- equipment racks, protective screens, and supply storage;
- small spectator or waiting area;
- instructor observation position;
- inactive, preparing, and active visual states;
- practice-safe summoning glyphs and impact markings;
- replaceable damaged-target and repaired-target variants;
- compact props that allow different lessons to redress the same ground.

### Water and Garden Kit

- narrow water-channel pieces with straights, corners, junctions, and endpoints;
- reflective or silver-water treatment if that exploration is retained;
- small pools or basins;
- island-edge waterfalls and mist at their drop-off;
- water-contact details such as foam, ripples, drains, bridges, and stepping
  surfaces;
- two or three tree families with multiple silhouettes and sizes;
- shrubs, reeds, flowers, vines, and low ground-cover clusters;
- planters, garden borders, trellises, and shaded seating;
- rocks and roots shaped for both open landscape and building edges.

## Tier 1 — Essential Character Assets

### Player and Major Recurring Characters

- campus-scale world sprites for each playable summoner who can be active in the
  hub;
- the four elemental teachers;
- Mr. Merriweather and Mrs. Merriweather;
- any additional general professor only if that role is retained explicitly;
- an Arena representative if the Arena needs an in-world host;
- an expedition coordinator only if departure requires a recurring character.

Each recurring character needs, at minimum:

- readable idle loop;
- walk loop if the character can relocate or participate in ambient movement;
- talk or gesture loop;
- one personality-defining ambient action where practical;
- consistent feet anchor, sprite scale, frame layout, and cutout margins;
- dialogue or Journal portrait only when the relevant UI actually displays one.

### Named Non-Teacher Hub Cast

The first content-ready hub should plan for approximately four to six named
non-teacher characters rather than expecting the faculty to originate most
quests. The exact identities require character and quest design, but the useful
role coverage is:

- two or three students with different social positions or interests;
- one campus operations or maintenance staff member;
- one research, archive, creature-care, or expedition-support staff member;
- one visitor, courier, alumnus, vendor, or other connection to the wider world.

These should be authored characters, not merely recolored crowd sprites.

### Ambient Population Kit

A modular population kit can provide more bodies than unique commissions:

- at least four student body silhouettes;
- several hair, clothing-color, and accessory variations;
- at least two non-faculty staff silhouettes;
- one campus security, steward, or safety-monitor silhouette if the world needs
  that role;
- paired conversation poses;
- seated reading or writing pose;
- carrying-books, parcels, or equipment pose;
- walking loop;
- simple reaction or greeting gesture;
- one or two small campus creatures, familiars, birds, or other ambient animals.

The target is enough combinable variation for roughly twelve to twenty visible
people without requiring twelve to twenty unique named-character commissions.

## Tier 1 — Everyday Activity Props

Liveliness depends on showing what people are doing. These small props will do
more work than additional empty buildings.

### Study and School Life

- books, scrolls, notebooks, loose papers, ink, and writing tools;
- satchels, stacked supplies, lesson boards, notice boards, and posted notices;
- benches, small tables, stools, outdoor desks, and shade canopies;
- club flyers, student-made signs, project displays, and practice schedules;
- cups, plates, food parcels, and other evidence of breaks between activities.

### Work and Maintenance

- brooms, buckets, hand tools, ladders, repair materials, and work cloths;
- carts, crates, barrels, parcels, and delivery bundles;
- watering tools, pruning tools, garden waste, and compost or soil containers;
- temporary barriers, warning markers, and active-repair variants;
- storage racks and covered supply piles.

### Expedition Activity

- packs, map cases, ropes, field journals, tents or folded shelters, and supply
  boxes;
- loading cart or luggage cluster;
- departure manifest or assignment board;
- magical navigation instrument or weather-reading device;
- returned-expedition clutter such as muddy equipment, samples, or damaged
  containers.

### Shop Activity

- exterior display table or shelving;
- packages, jars, cases, baskets, and bundled stock;
- shop sign and open/closed treatment;
- delivery handcart and receiving-area clutter;
- one or two magical merchandise-display effects that remain readable at hub
  scale.

## Tier 2 — Liveliness and Variation

### Ambient Character Actions

- walking short authored routes;
- talking in pairs or small groups;
- sitting, reading, writing, eating, or resting;
- carrying supplies between two sensible campus locations;
- sweeping, repairing, gardening, stocking, or posting notices;
- watching or reacting to activity in the Practice Ground;
- browsing outside the Shop;
- packing or returning equipment at the departure point;
- interacting with a familiar or small creature;
- brief magical practice that does not compete visually with quest markers.

### Environmental Motion

- moving cloud layers;
- water-channel flow, waterfalls, ripples, and mist;
- flags, hanging fabric, wind chimes, foliage, and loose papers responding to
  air movement;
- drifting petals, pollen, motes, or small magical particles;
- birds, insects, or distant flying silhouettes;
- subtle floating or orbiting behavior for selected magical infrastructure;
- Practice Ground activation and cooldown effects.

### Campus State Variants

- alternate notice-board postings;
- occupied and unoccupied seating arrangements;
- delivery-day Shop clutter;
- Practice Ground lesson setups using the same core kit;
- expedition departure and return setups;
- repaired, under-repair, and completed versions of a small number of props;
- quest-state overlays that change a location without replacing its whole art
  set.

## Tier 2 — Audio

- campus exploration music;
- high-altitude wind and cloud ambience;
- water-channel and waterfall loops;
- low crowd murmur with sparse student and staff details;
- footsteps for primary campus surfaces;
- cloth, foliage, chime, and hanging-sign movement;
- Practice Ground impacts, target hits, summoning activation, and reset sounds;
- Shop entrance or service cue;
- departure-point activation cue;
- small-creature, bird, or familiar sounds;
- restrained magical ambience for selected landmarks.

## Tier 2 — Interaction and Wayfinding Art

- final talk, quest-available, quest-ready, and current-objective markers;
- Shop, Arena, Practice Ground, and departure-point identifiers;
- campus direction signs or compact map-board art;
- interactable-object highlight treatment;
- Travel-waypoint presentation that belongs in the world;
- consistent locked, inactive, available, and active location states;
- neutral markers suitable for students, staff, and other quest sources rather
  than professor-specific iconography.

## Tier 3 — Later Polish

- time-of-day or weather variants if the game later supports them;
- additional crowd clothing and hair sets;
- more ambient creature species;
- rare cloud formations and distant flying events;
- seasonal plants, banners, student projects, or celebrations;
- named-character-specific sitting, working, or spellcasting animations;
- limited building-interior establishing images if a later story truly requires
  one;
- bespoke quest-state transformations for major campus events;
- multiplayer emotes and social-presence cosmetics when that phase begins.

## Explicit Non-Requirements

The current direction does not require the following merely to make the hub
feel complete:

- four elemental classroom buildings;
- four miniature elemental biomes on campus;
- a large lecture-hall exterior and interior;
- enterable interiors for every visible building;
- a unique building for every teacher or quest giver;
- unique art for every ambient student;
- a fully animated flight between campus and every excursion;
- a large open city beyond the bounded hub;
- elemental recolors applied indiscriminately across campus architecture.

## Decisions Needed Before Final Commission Briefs

These decisions do not block preliminary concept work, but they control exact
asset counts and dimensions:

- final campus art style, render scale, and sprite-sheet conventions;
- island silhouette and the amount of its underside visible during play;
- campus path and landmark layout;
- final form of the Practice Ground and expedition departure point;
- whether reflective or silver water is retained;
- the four teachers' designs, personalities, and ordinary campus habits;
- the first named student and staff quest-source roster;
- whether an additional general professor remains part of the Academy;
- which supporting buildings are functional and which only establish place;
- whether the hub presents only the active summoner or later shows other player
  characters through shared presence.

## Suggested Commission Bundles

1. **Style and scale sheet:** camera mockup, character scale, building scale,
   feet anchors, palette, line treatment, and animation template.
2. **Terrain and sky:** ground, paths, island edges, underside, clouds, and
   water.
3. **Architecture kit:** shared modular exterior pieces and Academy identity
   language.
4. **Core landmarks:** Shop, Practice Ground, Arena entrance, departure point,
   and campus identity landmark.
5. **Campus population:** player hub sprites, teachers, Merriweathers, named
   students and staff, then ambient population variants.
6. **Activity dressing:** study, maintenance, Shop, Practice Ground, and
   expedition prop kits.
7. **Motion and atmosphere:** ambient animation, VFX, audio, and state variants.
