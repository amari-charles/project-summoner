## What are the games that are most similar in gameplay to Fateforged? (this doesn’t have to align with Art Direction or visuals in general)

- **Clash Royale** is the primary gameplay comparison because battles happen in
  real time and the player actively deploys cards during the fight, rather than
  setting everything up for a fully automated battle.
- **Mini Warriors Reborn!** informed the scale of the armies, the side-on
  battlefield view, and the feeling of large forces clashing at once.
- **Cult of the Lamb** is a reference for the broader progression structure
  around battles, rather than for the combat itself.

## What are the games that are most similar in UI design to Fateforged? (e.g. Color Palette, Element Shapes etc.- doesn’t have to align with Gameplay)

**The Plucky Squire** is a good UI reference. Its color palette, especially its
range of bright and warm colors, is particularly relevant. Fateforged's fantasy
centers heavily on magic, so the UI should carry a magical or mystical feeling.
I generally prefer softer shapes over sharp or strongly angular ones, though
this is not a strict rule. Frames and panels can still have character and
detail, but I would prefer to avoid an overly ornate look.

## What are the games that are most similar in art style to Fateforged? (doesn’t have to align with gameplay and UI design)

**Don't Kill Them All** is a strong reference for the hand-drawn linework that
matches the direction established for Fateforged, as well as for its 2.5D
presentation.

## What is the setting of the game world? (modern, medieval, 80s, fantasy, combination etc.)

Fateforged is set in a high-fantasy world with a mystical tone and a
pre-industrial material culture, centered around a magical academy where
summoners study magic and command elemental creatures. Magic is an established
part of the world rather than something hidden or rare. The wider world is
shaped by powerful elemental forces, with regions defined by extreme biomes and
weather, such as frozen plains and volcanic landscapes filled with fire and
magma.

## Are some recognizable UI elements desired (e.g. UI elements that look like a book, scroll, tablet screen, stone etc.)

The Shop should evoke a physical shop that belongs in Fateforged's setting. It
could resemble a market stall, a caravan, an antique-style shop, or another
fitting interpretation at the designer's discretion. The Trait Tree should
feel mystical and arcane; its connected nodes could evoke an arcane diagram,
constellation, sigil, or summoning circle. The Quest Journal could resemble a
real journal, but that is not a requirement. Beyond those examples, the
interfaces do not need to resemble specific physical objects, but I am open to
recognizable elements where they make sense for the screen.
