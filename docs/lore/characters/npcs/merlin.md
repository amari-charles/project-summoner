# Headmaster Merlin

**Role:** Mentor and guide to the player
**Position:** Headmaster of the Academy of Summoning Arts

---

## Personality

- Wise
- Patient
- Formal but caring
- Embodies the Academy's "warm surface, hard truths" philosophy

*To be expanded in Phase 4*

---

## Appearance

**Classic wizard archetype** — the traditional Gandalf/Dumbledore mold.

**Key Features:**
- Long, flowing beard (white or silver)
- Ornate robes befitting the Headmaster position
- Staff or wand as symbol of office
- Aged but vital appearance
- Eyes that hold ancient knowledge

**Style Notes:**
- Should feel timeless and authoritative
- Warm colors to match the Academy's welcoming facade
- Details that hint at deep magical knowledge
- Neither frail nor imposing—balanced presence

---

## Portrait Art Direction

Dignified, knowing expression. Eyes that have seen much and judge wisely. Should radiate both warmth and expectation—welcoming but assessing. A mentor who believes in you, but whose standards you sense you must meet.

---

## Speech Patterns

**Formal, mystical, measured.**

### Vocabulary
- "Initiate" (formal address for students)
- "The art of summoning" (never just "magic")
- "Bind/bound" (for summoning companions)
- "All choices endure" (permanence theme)
- "Trial," "test," "proving" (structured progression)

### Tone
- Elevated language without being pretentious
- Encouragement tempered with expectation
- Mystical references grounded in practical wisdom

---

## Sample Quotes

> "Choose wisely, for in the art of summoning, all choices endure."

> "Remember, initiate: what you bind, stays bound. Choose your partner carefully."

### Act 1: The Initiate's Path
- Welcomes player to the Academy
- Helps new summoners understand the responsibilities of their existing affinity
- Guides early summoning and binding
- Oversees early trials
