# Headmaster Merlin

**Role:** Mentor and guide to the player
**Position:** Headmaster of the Academy of Summoning Arts

---

## Personality

- Wise
- Patient
- Formal but caring

*To be expanded in Phase 4*

---

## Speech Patterns

**Formal, mystical, measured.**

### Vocabulary
- "Initiate" (formal address for students)
- "The art of summoning" (never just "magic")
- "Bind/bound" (for summoning companions)
- "All choices endure" (permanence theme)
- "Trial," "test," "proving" (structured progression)

### Tone
- Elevated language without being pretentious
- Encouragement tempered with expectation
- Mystical references grounded in practical wisdom
