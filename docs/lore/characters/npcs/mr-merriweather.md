# Mr. Merriweather — Caravan Merchant

**Role:** Traveling merchant, caravan proprietor
**Partner:** Mrs. Merriweather

---

## Personality

Impossibly friendly. Warm, welcoming, and genuinely delighted to meet every customer. His enthusiasm is so authentic-seeming that it would be absurd to think he could be anything but what he appears: a kind-hearted merchant who truly loves helping young summoners succeed.

And yet.

The Merriweathers run a profitable operation. They sell to both sides of any conflict. They always seem to show up exactly when you need them most—and their prices reflect it. But you'd never suspect any of this from talking to Mr. Merriweather. He's just too... nice.

**Key Traits:**
- Relentlessly cheerful
- Makes every customer feel special
- Proud of his "carefully curated" wares
- Never a bad word about anyone
- Somehow always knows what you need

---

## Speech Patterns

Warm, enthusiastic, slightly theatrical. Uses lots of exclamations and terms of endearment.

**Vocabulary:**
- "Young summoner" (address for customers)
- "Why, hello there!"
- "What wonderful timing!"
- "Carefully curated"
- "Fair prices" (always mentioned)
- "Such a joy to help"

**Tone:**
- Welcoming without being pushy
- Genuinely interested in your journey
- Never directly sells—just "shares" his wares

## Portrait Art Direction

Warm smile, open posture, hands slightly raised in welcoming gesture. Eyes twinkling with genuine-seeming delight. Nothing about him should read as suspicious—that's the point.

---

## Relationship with Mrs. Merriweather

The perfect partnership. He handles the charm and customer relations; she handles... everything else. They finish each other's sentences and share knowing glances that customers interpret as sweet couple behavior.

---

## Role in Story

Appears at Caravan nodes throughout the campaign. Always has what you need. Always happy to see you. Always profitable.
