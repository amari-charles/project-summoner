# Mrs. Merriweather — Campus Shop Owner

**Role:** Campus Shop co-owner and operations lead
**Partner:** Mr. Merriweather

---

## Personality

Where Mr. Merriweather is warm and effusive, Mrs. Merriweather is warm and steady. She has that calming presence of someone who's genuinely happy to help—the kind of person you'd trust with your grandmother's jewelry. Patient, attentive, remembers details about every customer.

She handles "the practical side" of the business while her husband charms the customers. That includes the less-than-official arrangements — she's the one you talk to if you need something that's not on display. But you'd never know it from her demeanor. She's just so... nice.

**Key Traits:**
- Calming, maternal presence
- Remembers every customer's name and story
- Practical and organized
- Quietly competent
- Nothing escapes her notice

---

## Speech Patterns

Gentle, measured, reassuring. Speaks less than her husband but every word matters.

**Vocabulary:**
- "Dear" (address for customers)
- "Of course"
- "Let me see what we have"
- "My husband tells me..." (deflection)
- "Safe travels"

**Tone:**
- Softer than her husband
- Genuinely interested in your wellbeing
- Asks follow-up questions
- Never seems to be selling anything

Kind, knowing smile. Eyes that really see you. Slightly more reserved posture than her husband—hands perhaps clasped or holding something practical. Radiates trustworthiness.

---

## Relationship with Mr. Merriweather

They're a team in every sense. He's the face of the operation; she handles everything behind the curtain — including the stuff that doesn't make it onto the ledger. They communicate in glances and half-sentences, perfectly in sync after years together.

When Mr. Merriweather is being especially theatrical, she'll share a fond, exasperated look with customers—inviting them into the joke. It's endearing. It makes you like them both more.

---

## Role in Story

Runs the permanent Campus Shop alongside her husband. She handles its
transactions, inventory, and less-official sourcing while Mr. Merriweather
leads customer conversation. The shop is their permanent place in Academy life.
