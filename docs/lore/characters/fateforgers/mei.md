# Mei — Wind Fateforger

**Element:** Wind
**Gender:** Woman
**Ethnicity:** Asian

---

## Personality

Elusive and self-interested. Does what she wants and only what she wants—not out of coldness or dismissiveness, but because she's drawn to novelty above all else.

She forms brief but intense fascinations with people or things, then drifts away once the mystery is solved. Not flaky—she just doesn't see the point of lingering.

Her standards are high. Average summoners don't interest her. Even above-average isn't enough. Only the exceptional catch her attention: unusual abilities she hasn't seen, genuine unpredictability, hidden depths to uncover, or near-peak strength. The other Fateforgers are among the few at the academy who can actually hold her interest.

A loner by nature—not because she dislikes people, but because she can't be caught hold of.

---

## Motivation

Self-improvement is its own reward. She's at the academy to gather what she needs to become stronger, always pushing toward new limits. Not particularly attached to the school or interested in staying longer than necessary.

One of the strongest students in her year.

---

## Portrait Art Direction

Looking to the side, elsewhere—as if the viewer isn't interesting enough to hold her attention.
