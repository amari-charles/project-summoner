# Kai Ise — Space Fateforger

**Element:** None (Space Spirit companion)
**Gender:** Man
**Ethnicity:** Asian

---

## Concept

Kai Ise is a man who has been isekai'd from Earth to this new world. He woke up with no elemental affinity—a fraud with no real magic powers.

However, a **Space Spirit** found him and became his companion. Kai pretends to have magic by having his Space Spirit spawn things for him. Nobody knows his secret.

---

## Gameplay Identity

Slight buff to all elements. The implied reason (never stated explicitly) is that his Space Spirit chooses the stronger summons.

Jack of all trades, master of none—but with hidden advantages.

---

## Appearance

- White tee
- Trackpants
- Slides
- Space Spirit sits on his shoulder

---

## Personality

Appears **strong and confident** on the surface.

But in the card art, there will be brief flashes of anxiety:
- Chattering teeth
- Eyes darting around
- Nervous tells that happen so fast you're not sure if you saw them

The confident facade masks someone who's in way over his head.
