# Narrative Arc

The story of Fateforged follows a summoner's journey from novice initiate to master of elemental forces.

---

## Core Themes

### 1. Meaningful Choice
Decisions have permanence. "What you bind, stays bound." "All choices endure."

Every card selection and every companion bound helps define who the summoner
becomes. There are no take-backs. Elemental affinity is part of the summoner,
not a choice or power granted by the Academy.

### 2. Growth Through Trial
Progress comes through structured tests of skill and understanding. The Academy doesn't hand out power—it must be earned through demonstrated mastery.

### 3. Elemental Connection
Affinity to elemental forces is inherent, personal, and defining. A fire
summoner isn't just using fire—they *are* fire in some fundamental way. The
Academy teaches summoners how to understand and use what is already theirs.

### 4. Mastery Through Practice
Theory must translate to practical application. Knowledge alone is insufficient; the battlefield reveals true understanding.

---

## Act 1: The Initiate's Path

**Arc Summary**: The player chooses a summoner whose affinity is already part of
them, enters Academy life, binds companions, and develops mastery through
practice and consequential choices.

### Event 1: Summoner Selection
- **Function**: Choose which established summoner's path the player will follow
- **Key Principle**: The selected summoner already possesses their elemental affinity
- **Outcome**: The player's summoner identity is selected; no affinity is revealed or unlocked

### Event 2: First Summon
- **Function**: Teach permanence of choices, introduce companion bonding
- **Key Moment**: "Remember, initiate: what you bind, stays bound. Choose your partner carefully."
- **Outcome**: Player selects their first card/companion

### Battle 1: The First Trial
- **Function**: Test practical application; transition from selection to action
- **Context**: The summoner applies established abilities and newly bound
  companions in controlled practice.
- **Stakes**: Prove combat readiness and earn place among Academy summoners
