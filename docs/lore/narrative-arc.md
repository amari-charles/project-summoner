# Narrative Arc

The story of Fateforged follows a summoner's journey from novice initiate to master of elemental forces.

---

## Core Themes

### 1. Meaningful Choice
Decisions have permanence. "What you bind, stays bound." "All choices endure."

Every card selection, every affinity choice, every companion bound—these define who the summoner becomes. There are no take-backs.

### 2. Growth Through Trial
Progress comes through structured tests of skill and understanding. The Academy doesn't hand out power—it must be earned through demonstrated mastery.

### 3. Elemental Connection
Affinity to elemental forces is personal and defining. A fire summoner isn't just using fire—they *are* fire in some fundamental way.

### 4. Mastery Through Practice
Theory must translate to practical application. Knowledge alone is insufficient; the battlefield reveals true understanding.

---

## Act 1: The Initiate's Path

**Arc Summary**: Player arrives at the Academy, discovers their affinity, binds their first companion, and proves combat readiness through initial trials.

### Event 1: Trial of Affinities
- **Function**: Establish setting, introduce Headmaster Merlin, discover elemental connection
- **Key Moment**: "Choose wisely, for in the art of summoning, all choices endure."
- **Outcome**: Player selects their elemental affinity

### Event 2: First Summon
- **Function**: Teach permanence of choices, introduce companion bonding
- **Key Moment**: "Remember, initiate: what you bind, stays bound. Choose your partner carefully."
- **Outcome**: Player selects their first card/companion

### Battle 1: The First Trial
- **Function**: Test practical application; transition from selection to action
- **Context**: "Your affinity chosen, your companion bound—Headmaster Merlin now calls you to the training grounds."
- **Stakes**: Prove combat readiness and earn place among Academy summoners
