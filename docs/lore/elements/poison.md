# Poison

**Category:** Outer Element

## Concept

The element of corruption and persistence. Represents decay, mutation, and inevitability.

Poison works slowly but surely. It does not confront strength directly—it undermines it from within.

## Gameplay Identity

**Attrition, damage over time, weakening**

Poison summons wear enemies down over time. They apply lingering effects that slowly destroy.

- **Damage over time** — Continuous health drain
- **Attrition** — Wins through accumulated damage, not burst
- **Weakening effects** — Debuffs that reduce enemy power
- **Lingering presence** — Effects persist long after application

## Philosophy

Represents patience, transformation through corruption, and the breaking down of barriers. Poison is change that cannot be stopped, only slowed.
