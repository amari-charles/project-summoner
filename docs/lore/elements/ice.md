# Ice

**Elevated from:** Water

## Concept

Flow becomes control and stillness. Preservation through perfection.

Ice is water that has chosen permanence over adaptability. Where water flows around obstacles, ice reshapes the world to its will through patience and precision.

## Gameplay Identity

**Control, slows, punishment**

Ice summons reduce enemy effectiveness. They punish speed and mobility with freezing control.

- **Slows** — Reduces enemy movement and attack speed
- **Control-focused** — Locks down threats, prevents escapes
- **Punishes movement** — Enemies that rely on speed suffer most
- **Reduces effectiveness** — Makes enemies weaker, not just damaged

## Philosophy

Represents control, preservation, and clarity. Ice does not destroy—it holds all things in perfect stillness until the moment of release.
