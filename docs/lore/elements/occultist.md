# Occultist

**Category:** Occultist Element

## Concept

The systemic inversion of all other forces—corruption, manipulation, and forbidden knowledge.

Occultist is not an element in the natural sense. It is the force that disrupts and nullifies elemental laws, bending reality through means that should not exist.

## Gameplay Identity

**Rule-breaking, forbidden power**

Occultist forces break the normal rules of the game. They cheat the system itself.

- **Rule-breaking** — Ignores normal mechanics and restrictions
- **Forbidden power** — Does things other elements cannot
- **Antagonist element** — Primarily used by enemies, not player summons
- **System corruption** — Disrupts the elemental order itself

## Philosophy

Represents forbidden power, corruption, and the breaking of natural order. Occultist forces serve as antagonists—they are what happens when someone reaches beyond the limits of the elemental system.
