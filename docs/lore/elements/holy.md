# Holy

**Elevated from:** Fire

## Concept

Flame becomes sanctity. Energy with purpose, divine intention, and cleansing light.

Holy is not merely purified fire—it is fire that has transcended physical existence to embody moral truth. Where fire transforms matter, holy transforms meaning.
