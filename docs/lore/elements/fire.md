# Fire

## Gameplay Identity

**Erratic, burst damage, powerful up front**

Fire fateforgers hit hard and fast. Their strength is in explosive opening plays and unpredictable bursts of power. They overwhelm opponents before they can mount a defense.

## Philosophy

Embodies vitality, passion, and transformation. Fire is not merely destruction—it is change itself.

## Variant: Solar

Controlled, radiant heat. A tempered but fierce energy.
