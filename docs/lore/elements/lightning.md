# Lightning

**Category:** Outer Element

## Concept

The element of pure energy, speed, and precision. Represents intensity and insight.

Lightning strikes in an instant and leaves nothing unchanged. It is the element of revelation—truth delivered in a flash.

## Gameplay Identity

**Speed, stuns, burst windows**

Lightning summons strike with sharp, fast impact. High tempo with powerful burst windows.

- **Speed** — Fast attacks, quick execution
- **Stuns** — Interrupts and disables enemies briefly
- **Burst windows** — Short periods of intense damage output
- **High tempo, less sustain** — Powerful spikes but runs out of steam

## Philosophy

Represents sudden clarity, raw power, and the moment of impact. Lightning does not negotiate or adapt—it arrives.
