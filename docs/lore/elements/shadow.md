# Shadow

**Category:** Outer Element

## Concept

The unseen, deceptive force. Governs secrecy, illusion, and reflection.

Shadow exists only in relation to light—it is the absence that defines presence. It reveals what others hide and hides what others reveal.

*To be developed*

## Philosophy

Represents hidden truth, misdirection, and the power of what is not seen. Shadow is not evil—it is simply what exists beyond the light.
