# Shadow

**Category:** Outer Element

## Concept

The unseen, deceptive force. Governs secrecy, illusion, and reflection.

Shadow exists only in relation to light—it is the absence that defines presence. It reveals what others hide and hides what others reveal.

## Gameplay Identity

**Stealth, illusions, assassination**

Shadow summons strike from the unseen. They deceive and eliminate key targets.

- **Stealth and ambush** — Hidden until attacking, surprise strikes
- **Clones and illusions** — Decoys that confuse enemy targeting
- **Assassination** — High single-target burst, eliminates priority threats
- **Misdirection** — Enemies never know what's real

## Philosophy

Represents hidden truth, misdirection, and the power of what is not seen. Shadow is not evil—it is simply what exists beyond the light.
