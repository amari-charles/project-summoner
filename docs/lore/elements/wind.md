# Wind

## Gameplay Identity

**Fast, elusive, flowy**

Wind fateforgers are hard to pin down. Their strength is in speed and evasion—striking before opponents can react and slipping away before they can retaliate.

## Philosophy

Represents motion, freedom, and volatility. Wind cannot be held.

## Variant: Tempest

Chaotic, fast, and unpredictable. Wind unleashed.
