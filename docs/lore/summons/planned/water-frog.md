# Water Frog

**Element:** Water
**Status:** Ready for art

## Concept

A frog that hops around the battlefield, lashing out with its long tongue to pull enemies.

## Gameplay Ideas

- **Jumping movement** — Hops between positions (spine simulated)
- **Tongue pull** — Ranged attack that pulls enemies toward it
- Fits Water's adaptive/flexible identity through mobility and disruption

## Artist Notes

- **Shape:** Frog silhouette (compact, round)
- **Color:** Blue/teal
- **Paid animation:** Tongue attack (tongue extends out, pulls back)
- **Spine handled:** Hop movement, idle
