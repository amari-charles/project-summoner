# Pollen Spreader

**Element:** Life
**Status:** Concept

## Concept

A creature that spreads pollen as it moves, spawning new units or buffing allies.

## Gameplay Ideas

- **Spreads pollen** — Leaves pollen trail or cloud as it moves
- **Spawns units** — Pollen becomes small plant units over time
- Or buffs allies who pass through pollen
- Mobile replication engine
