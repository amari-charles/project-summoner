# Wind Swordsman

**Element:** Wind
**Status:** Ready for art

## Concept

A small robed figure wielding a sword. Dashes through enemies with lightning-fast slashes.

## Gameplay Ideas

- **Dash attack** — Rushes forward and slashes
- **Dodge chance** — Small chance to evade incoming attacks
- Fits Wind's elusive/fast identity
