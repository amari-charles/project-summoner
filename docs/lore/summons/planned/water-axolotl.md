# Water Axolotl

**Element:** Water
**Status:** Concept

## Concept

A regenerating aquatic salamander that cleanses and sustains allies.

## Gameplay Ideas

- **Slow heal over time** — Passively heals nearby allies, steady sustain
- **Debuff cleanse** — Periodically removes all debuffs from nearby allies (~10 seconds)
- **Simple attack** — Bite or tail slap, not its main contribution
- Support/cleanser role — Value is sustain and cleanse, not damage
- Fits Water's "go with the flow" identity — washes away negative effects

## Visual Notes

- Distinctive axolotl silhouette (frilly external gills)
- Cute, smiley appearance
- Flowing, aquatic movement
