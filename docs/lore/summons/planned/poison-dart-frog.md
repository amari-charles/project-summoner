**Element:** Poison
**Status:** Concept

## Concept

A small, brightly colored frog that secretes deadly toxins.

## Gameplay Ideas

- Applies poison on attack
- Small and quick
- High threat despite small size
- Bright warning colors (gameplay signal: dangerous!)

- Bright, warning coloration (reds, yellows, blues)
