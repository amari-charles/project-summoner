# Venus Flytrap

**Element:** Life
**Status:** Concept

## Concept

A carnivorous plant that snaps at enemies who get too close.

## Gameplay Ideas

- **Stationary** — Doesn't move, placed as area denial
- **Snap attack** — Bites enemies that enter range
- Punishes enemies who approach
- One of few stationary Life units

## Visual Notes

- Classic venus flytrap silhouette (hinged jaw-like leaves)
- Rooted in place
