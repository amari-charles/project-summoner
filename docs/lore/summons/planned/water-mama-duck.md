# Mama Duck

**Element:** Water
**Status:** Concept

## Concept

A mother duck accompanied by baby ducklings. They fight as a coordinated unit.

## Gameplay Ideas

- **Coordinated targeting** — All ducks focus the same target
- **Mama is melee** — Front-line protector
- **Ducklings are ranged** — Back-line damage dealers
- Possible mechanics:
  - Ducklings spawn with mama
  - Mama buffs or protects ducklings
  - Ducklings die easily but mama is tanky
  - All attack whoever mama targets
