# Wind Hummingbird

**Element:** Wind
**Status:** Concept

## Concept

An extremely fast bird that attacks multiple times in quick succession.

## Gameplay Ideas

- **Extremely fast** — Highest speed unit
- **Multi-attack** — Hits multiple times per attack cycle
- **Hard to hit** — Evasive, difficult to target
- Fits Wind's elusive/fast identity

## Visual Notes

- Hummingbird silhouette
- Small, rapid wing movement
- Blur effects to convey speed
