# Seed Runner

**Element:** Life
**Status:** Concept

## Concept

A fast-moving seed that plants itself on death, spawning a bigger unit.

## Gameplay Ideas

- **High mobility** — Fast, runs around the battlefield
- **Death trigger** — When killed, plants itself and spawns a larger unit
- Delayed payoff — Investment now, value later
- Fits Life's growth/replication identity

## Visual Notes

- Small seed silhouette with legs or rolling movement
- Compact, quick
