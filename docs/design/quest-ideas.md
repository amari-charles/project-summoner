# Quest and World Activity Ideas

**Status:** Experimental — no idea in this document is accepted product direction
**Type:** Quest, exploration, and map-activity workbench
**Started:** 2026-08-26

## Purpose

Capture possible things the player could discover and do while exploring the
Academy and wider world. These concepts may be changed, combined, or discarded.
They do not alter the accepted rules in [Quest System](quest-system.md) unless a
decision is explicitly approved and promoted into the relevant source-of-truth
document.

## Mama Duck — Hatch the Flock

**Status:** Experimental

### Core Idea

The player collects physical eggs in the explorable world for the Mama Duck
card. The eggs they choose to collect and hatch determine which duckling
followers Mama Duck brings into battle.

### Player Activity and Agency

- Search locations on the map for different eggs.
- Decide which eggs to collect and hatch.
- Build Mama Duck's follower group through those choices rather than receiving
  one predetermined set of ducklings.

### Open Questions

- Does this quest unlock Mama Duck, modify an already-owned card, or both?
- What distinguishes one egg and resulting duckling from another?
- Are hatching choices permanent, replaceable, or part of a larger upgrade
  branch?
- How many ducklings can accompany Mama Duck at once?

## Modular Elemental Worm

**Status:** Experimental

### Existing Documentation

The [Product Direction Log](../project/direction-log.md) already uses a
segmented worm summon as an example of accepted permanent behavioral upgrade
branches: one branch could split the worm on death, while another could let it
lose a segment and survive lethal damage.

It did **not** previously document the modular elemental-segment concept below.

### Core Idea

The worm is made of individually augmentable body segments. Each segment can
have a different element, and the player collects the materials needed to
augment each segment.

### Player Activity and Agency

- Find the materials required for different elemental augmentations.
- Choose which individual segment to augment with each element.
- Gradually author the worm's elemental composition through exploration and
  material choices.

### Open Questions

- How does a segment's element change the worm in battle?
- Can an augmented segment be changed later?
- Does segment order matter, or only the number of segments of each element?
- How does this modular system relate to the already accepted mutually
  exclusive split-versus-survive behavioral branch?
- Is the worm acquired through one quest and augmented through later quests, or
  developed through a single quest chain?
