# Academy World Definition

**Status:** Accepted product and worldbuilding decisions
**Type:** Source of truth for locked Academy-world facts
**Last Updated:** 2026-08-24

## Purpose

Record only the Academy geography, campus, and place decisions the user has
explicitly accepted. Candidate names, layouts, histories, and explanations do
not belong here until approved.

Exploratory material lives in
[Academy World Explorations](academy-world-explorations.md). An idea appearing
there does not make it canon.

## Institutional Identity

- The Academy of Summoning Arts is a bright, welcoming magical institution with
  a quietly ruthless meritocratic core.
- Its material culture is broadly pre-industrial, while magic can support
  sophisticated institutions, craft, and infrastructure.
- A summoner's elemental affinity is inherent. The Academy does not reveal,
  grant, awaken, select, or unlock affinity.

## Campus Form

- The Academy's central campus is a compact, bounded, top-down walkable home
  base. It is not an open world or a destination menu.
- Buildings and landmarks are discrete placeable locations. The campus can
  change without repainting one monolithic background.
- The exact geographic region, terrain, architecture, history, population,
  districts, and map are not yet decided.
- Placeholder scene positions and inherited graybox names do not establish
  canon geography.

## Required Campus Functions

### Campus Shop

- The Academy has a permanent Campus Shop.
- Mr. and Mrs. Merriweather own and operate it.
- Its building form and location are not yet decided.

### Elemental Teaching Places

- Fire, Water, Earth, and Wind each have an associated teacher.
- Each elemental teacher has a recognizable place on campus where they
  regularly post up, meet students, and hold classes.
- These places must feel like working parts of the Academy, not merely quest
  markers.
- A teaching place may be a hall, workshop, courtyard, garden, terrace,
  pavilion, field, or another suitable environment. It does not automatically
  require a conventional classroom building.
- The four teaching places do not need to form symmetrical elemental quarters
  or miniature biomes.

### Elemental Biomes

- Fire, Water, Earth, and Wind each need a biome or field environment for
  element-specific world and gameplay content.
- Their locations and their relationship to the central hub are not yet
  decided.
- Campus layout work must leave room to connect these biomes coherently later;
  it must not assume they are all embedded inside the central campus.

### Other Accepted Functions

- Competitive play has a physical Arena presence on campus. Its form, name, and
  location are open.
- A covert underground area is an accepted direction for illicit card cracking
  and progression rituals. Its origin, entrance, layout, and exact institutional
  status are open.
- Professors and other recurring characters can occupy recognizable outdoor
  landmarks rather than requiring dedicated interiors.

## Buildings Versus Persistent UI

Spellbook, Journal, Inventory, Summoner Profile, Friends, and Settings are UI
tools. They do not require campus buildings that duplicate their functionality.
A physical place must have a world, character, teaching, social, discovery, or
gameplay reason to be visited.

## Retired Concepts

- The Crystal Chamber and Trial of Affinities are retired. They are not campus
  locations or Academy practices.
- Training Grounds, Mission Hall, Class Hall, and Dorms are inherited working
  labels, not accepted names or required buildings.
