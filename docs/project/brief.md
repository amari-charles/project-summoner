# Fateforged — Project Brief

*A studio-ready overview of the game*

---

## Elevator Pitch

**Fateforged** is a 1v1 real-time tactical battler where every card is single-use and every choice shapes your fate. Build your army during a preparation phase, then watch your forces clash autonomously. It's the strategic depth of a deckbuilder with the immediacy of Clash Royale—designed for mobile, built for mastery.

---

## Core Fantasy

You are a **wizard-commander** leading a living army into battle. Two armies face off, formations are set, and then chaos erupts. Victory comes not from outspending your opponent, but from **out-summoning** them—using timing, positioning, and the right cards at the right moment.

---

## Core Loop

```
Collect → Build → Battle → Reward → Evolve
```

1. **Collect** cards through campaign events and rewards
2. **Build** a 30-card deck around your chosen summoner
3. **Battle** in 3-5 minute real-time matches
4. **Reward** with gold, cards, and progression
5. **Evolve** your summoner and cards through meaningful choices

---

## Key Features

- **Two-Phase Battles**: 30-second preparation phase to build formations, then autonomous battle until victory
- **Single-Use Cards**: Every card in your deck is consumed when played—no infinite cycling
- **Permanent Choices**: Campaign events offer card choices that permanently close off alternatives
- **Elemental Summoners**: Four core elements (Fire, Water, Wind, Earth) with distinct playstyles
- **Army Rarity System**: Common cards spawn swarms (12 units), Legendary cards spawn one champion
- **No Pay-to-Win**: Monetization through cosmetics and summoner unlocks only

---

## What Makes It Different

| Competitor | Fateforged Difference |
|------------|----------------------|
| Clash Royale | Single-use cards, preparation phase, no lane system |
| Auto-battlers | Real-time card plays, not just positioning |
| Traditional CCGs | Real-time combat, not turn-based |

**Unique hook**: Your deck depletes as you play. The battle isn't about card advantage—it's about making every summon count.

---

## Target Audience

- **Primary**: Mobile strategy gamers (25-40) who enjoy tactical depth
- **Secondary**: PC/desktop players seeking quick competitive matches
- **Comparable audiences**: Clash Royale, Teamfight Tactics, Slay the Spire, Hades

---

## Visual Style

2.5D perspective with orthographic camera. Painterly, high-contrast fantasy with distinct elemental identities. Inspired by Cult of the Lamb's clarity and Mini Warriors' army scale.

**Tone**: Competitive yet mythic. Serious, mystical, and proud.

---

## Platform & Tech

- **Engine**: Godot 4.5
- **Primary Platform**: Mobile (iOS/Android)
- **Secondary Platform**: PC/Desktop
- **Session Length**: 3-5 minutes

---

## Business Model

- **Free-to-play** with cosmetic monetization
- **No pay-to-win**: All gameplay content earnable through play
- **Premium options**: Summoner unlocks, cosmetics, battle passes
- **Philosophy**: Monetization sells access to attempts, not power directly

---

## Current State

**Phase**: Alpha (Foundation Systems)

**Implemented**:
- Core combat with two-phase battle system
- Card playing with drag-and-drop
- Unit AI (melee, ranged, flying)
- Campaign progression with branching events
- Save/load system with cloud-ready architecture
- VFX system for abilities
- 4 elemental summoners with progression framework

**In Development**:
- Visual polish and game feel
- Additional unit types and cards
- Campaign content expansion
