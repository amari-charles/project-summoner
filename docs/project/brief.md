# Fateforged — Product Brief

**Updated:** 2026-08-24
**Status:** Pre-alpha

Fateforged is a 1v1 real-time tactical battler set at a magical Academy. Players
choose a summoner, build an elemental deck, summon creatures, cast spells, and
break the opposing summoner's base on a deterministic 3D battlefield.

## Player Experience

The walkable Academy campus is the player's home. Professors offer ordered quest
chains through dialogue and world interactions. Quest steps may open a reusable
encounter with a fixed, owned, or flexible loadout. Battle completion flows
through one Results experience, advances the quest, and returns the player to
the campus. Curriculum capacity makes accepting academic opportunities a
meaningful commitment.

The Merriweathers own the permanent Campus Shop. Purchases and resources are
account or summoner progression; there is no temporary run economy.

## Product Pillars

- Fast, readable real-time tactics with deterministic simulation.
- Distinct elemental identities expressed through cards, summoners, and traits.
- A physical Academy populated by professors, quests, and discoveries.
- Durable collection and progression choices tied to the selected summoner.
- Shared battle, reward, and Results contracts across quest and debug content.

## Current Scope

- Singleplayer authored encounters and direct Debug Arena battles.
- Deck building, card progression, summoner progression, traits, inventory, and
  the Campus Shop.
- Provider-neutral session boundaries that can support multiplayer work without
  coupling the simulation to networking.
