# Fateforged — Product Vision

**Updated:** 2026-08-24

Fateforged should feel like attending a living magical Academy whose lessons are
played through real-time tactical battles. The player meets professors in the
world, chooses which quest chains deserve limited curriculum capacity, prepares
a summoner and deck for each encounter, and brings durable rewards back to the
campus.

## The Fantasy

The player is not selecting nodes from a progression board. They are inhabiting
a school: talking to characters, discovering opportunities, accepting quests,
and proving their choices in battle. Professors and locations give the content
context; the Journal makes active commitments legible; encounters provide a
reusable bridge into combat.

## Strategic Identity

Elemental cards and summoner traits create recognizable play styles without
forcing one correct deck. Durable acquisition and curriculum choices should
make two players' collections and Academy experiences diverge over time.

## Architectural Consequence

Quest sequencing, encounter execution, battle authority, rewards, persistence,
and the shop have separate owners. New content should compose those boundaries
instead of creating a second progression flow.
