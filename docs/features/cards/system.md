# Card System

Cards are immutable catalog definitions plus durable ownership/progression data.
Summon cards create units or structures; spell cards apply immediate or lasting
effects through the battle command pipeline.

## Ownership and Decks

- Account-owned cards are available to eligible summoners.
- Summoner-bound cards belong only to that summoner.
- Deck validation enforces size, copies, ownership, and encounter restrictions.
- Fixed encounter loadouts replace player selection for that encounter only.
- Owned loadouts require a valid saved deck.
- Flexible loadouts let the encounter assemble or constrain a temporary deck.

## Progression

Cards gain XP from authoritative battle completion and level independently of
account currency. Rewards use the universal reward service and stable claim
identities.
