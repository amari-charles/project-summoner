# Summoner System

Summoners are durable player identities with their own progression, decks,
collection state, equipment, traits, quest progress, and authored-battle
receipts. The selected summoner supplies the player side of an encounter.

## Responsibilities

- Selection and ownership
- Level, XP, and stat progression
- Per-summoner decks, cards, items, and equipment
- Trait trees and authored identity
- Quest state, curriculum capacity, and encounter loadouts
- Battle-attempt and first-clear history

Account-wide resources and the Campus Shop remain outside summoner ownership.
