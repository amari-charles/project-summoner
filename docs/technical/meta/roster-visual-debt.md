# Roster Visual Debt

Status: working note
Last updated: 2026-05-29

This note tracks visual/presentation debt from the initial Fire, Water, Earth, and Wind roster pass. It does not define gameplay behavior.

## Placeholder Unit Scenes

- Fire roster placeholders: Cinder Caster, Ember Bomb Carrier, Kindling Swarm, Fire Frontliner, Overheat Brawler, Flame Channeler.
- Water roster placeholders: Water Redistributor, Slippery Melee, Water Ranged, Barbed Inflator.
- Earth roster placeholders: Earth Shield Support, Burrow Ambusher.
- Wind roster placeholders: Wind Diver, Wind Speed Support, Wind Miss Support, Wind Swarm, Flow Striker.

## Reused/Mismatched Existing Visuals

- Water Frontliner currently uses an Earth Sprite scene.
- Water Skimmer currently uses the Puff scene.
- Water Cleanser currently uses the Life Wisp scene.
- Taunt Pulse Guardian currently uses the Stone Ape scene.

## Reused Projectile Visuals

- Duckling, Water Skimmer, Water Ranged, Wind Pushback Unit, Wind Speed Support, and Wind Miss Support currently reuse `WindPuff` projectile visuals.

## Naming/Readability Debt

- Several functional placeholders still have descriptive engineering names instead of final fantasy names, such as Earth Bullet Unit, Wind Pushback Unit, Wind Speed Support, Wind Miss Support, Water Ranged, and Water Frontliner.
