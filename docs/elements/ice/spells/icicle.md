# Icicle

**Element:** Ice
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 2
- **Damage:** 30
- **Frost Stacks:** Applies frost stacks
- **Slow:** 30% for 3 seconds
- **Target:** Single unit

## Effect

Cheap single-target damage with frost stack application. Projectile hits target, applies frost stacks and slows them.

## Visual

- Icy shard projectile
- Frost particles on impact
- Target gets slight blue tint while slowed

## Tactical Use

- Cheap harass and stack building
- Slow enemies to kite or escape
- Build toward freeze threshold (30 stacks)
- Combo setup for Shatter

## Stacking

Part of the Frost stacking system. At 30 frost stacks, targets become frozen solid.
