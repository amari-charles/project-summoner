# Freeze

**Element:** Ice
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 4
- **Freeze Duration:** 2 seconds
- **AOE Radius:** 5 units
- **Target:** Enemy units in area

## Effect

Hard CC spell that DIRECTLY applies frozen state (skips stack buildup). Enemies in area are frozen solid - cannot move, cannot attack, cannot take damage.

## Visual

- Targets encased in ice crystals
- Frozen in place, no movement
- Shatter effect when freeze ends
- Icy mist around frozen units

## Tactical Use

- Disable key targets instantly
- Buy time during bad fights
- Setup for Shatter combo damage
- Note: Frozen units are invulnerable - you're pausing them, not killing them

## Direct Threshold

This spell applies the frozen state directly without needing 30 frost stacks. Pay more mana for instant effect.
