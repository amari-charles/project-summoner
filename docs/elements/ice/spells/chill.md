# Chill

**Element:** Ice
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 4
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Zone Radius:** 7 units
- **Frost Stacks:** Applies frost stacks per second

## Effect

Zone control spell. Creates a frozen area that applies frost stacks over time. Enemies progressively slow the longer they stay in the zone.

## Visual

- Frozen ground texture
- Mist rising from the area
- Enemies accumulate visible frost the longer they stay
- Icy blue color palette

## Tactical Use

- Zone denial - enemies don't want to stay in it
- Stack builder for freeze threshold
- Extended fight zone favors you
- Combo with Shatter for AOE damage on slowed enemies

## Stacking

Applies frost stacks every second. Enemies who stay in the zone will eventually hit 30 stacks and freeze solid.
