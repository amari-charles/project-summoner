# Shatter

**Element:** Ice
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 3
- **Damage:** 70
- **AOE Radius:** 8 units
- **Condition:** Only damages FROZEN or SLOWED enemies

## Effect

Combo finisher spell. Deals high damage to all frozen or slowed enemies in the area. Enemies without frost effects take no damage.

## Visual

- Ice on targets explodes violently
- Sharp crystal fragments burst outward
- Cracking/shattering sound
- Ice particles scatter

## Tactical Use

- Combo with Freeze for burst damage
- Combo with Icicle/Chill for AOE damage on slowed enemies
- Punish clumped enemies after CC
- Rewards chaining ice spells together

## Combo Examples

- Freeze (4) + Shatter (3) = 7 mana for freeze + 70 damage burst
- Chill zone + Shatter = Damage all enemies caught in slow zone
