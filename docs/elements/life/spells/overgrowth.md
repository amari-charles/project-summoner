# Overgrowth

**Element:** Life
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 5
- **Duration:** 6 seconds
- **Zone Radius:** 7 units
- **Ally Buff:** +20% max HP
- **Enemy Effect:** Rooted (can attack, can't move)

## Effect

Powerful zone control. Creates an area of dense vegetation that buffs allies and roots enemies. Enemies can still attack but cannot move.

## Visual

- Dense vegetation erupts from ground
- Vines grab enemy feet, holding them in place
- Allies look more vigorous, enhanced
- Lush nature environment

## Tactical Use

- Lock down enemies while buffing your team
- Zone control favoring your side
- Pin melee units away from your backline
- Create advantageous fight locations

## Notes

Rooted enemies can still attack - they're held in place, not disabled. Ranged enemies can still fight effectively.
