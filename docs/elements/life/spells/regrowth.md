# Regrowth

**Element:** Life
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 4
- **Healing:** 20 HP/sec for 5 seconds (100 total)
- **Target:** Single allied unit

## Effect

Heal over time spell. Target allied unit regenerates health steadily over the duration. More total healing than Bloom but spread out.

## Visual

- Vines wrap gently around unit
- Steady green glow throughout duration
- Leaves flutter off periodically
- Nature embracing the unit

## Tactical Use

- More total healing than Bloom (100 vs 50)
- Pre-cast before engagement
- Sustain during extended fights
- Can't burst heal - plan ahead
