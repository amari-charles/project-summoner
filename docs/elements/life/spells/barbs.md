# Barbs

**Element:** Life
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 3
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **AOE Radius:** TBD
- **Reflect Damage:** 15 per hit

## Effect

Defensive buff. Allied units in the area grow thorny barbs. When enemies attack them, attackers take damage.

## Visual

- Spiky thorns grow on units' armor/skin
- Visible barbs covering unit
- Enemies flinch when they attack
- Small damage tick on each attacker

## Tactical Use

- Punish melee attackers
- Makes attacking your units costly
- Defensive buff that deals damage
- Counter to attack-speed focused enemies
