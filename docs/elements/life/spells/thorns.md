# Thorns

**Element:** Life
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 3
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Zone Radius:** 6 units
- **Damage:** 15/sec
- **Slow:** 20%

## Effect

Zone control spell. Creates a patch of thorns that damages and slows enemies passing through.

## Visual

- Spiky brambles erupt from ground
- Thorny vines covering the area
- Enemies visibly get caught and pricked
- Nature hazard aesthetic

## Tactical Use

- Zone denial - punish enemies for walking through
- Control pathways and chokepoints
- Slow + damage combo
- Area control during fights
