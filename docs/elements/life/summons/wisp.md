# Wisp

**Element:** Life
**Status:** Implemented

## Overview

A common creature native to the elemental plane of life. Wisps are among the most frequently summoned creatures due to their reliability and ease of bonding with novice summoners. They exist across all elemental planes in variant forms, each shaped by their element's nature.

## Visual Description

- Teardrop-shaped body made of living essence
- Organic and soft - surface seems to breathe
- Hovers above the ground, body glowing warmly
- Soft green/gold with occasional flower-like blooms
- No distinct features - pure elemental energy

## Gameplay Role

- **Type:** Melee
- **Archetype:** Basic attacker
- Straightforward combat unit with minimal abilities
- Embodies life's philosophy: nurturing and resilient
