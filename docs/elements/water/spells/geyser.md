# Geyser

**Element:** Water
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 4
- **Damage:** 70
- **AOE Radius:** 6 units
- **Cast Type:** Ground target with delay

## Effect

Ground-targeted AOE burst. After a brief delay, water erupts violently from the target location dealing damage to all enemies in radius.

## Visual

- Ground bubbles briefly (telegraph)
- Water erupts upward violently
- Splash and spray particles
- Blue/white color palette

## Tactical Use

- Cheaper than Fireball (4 vs 5 mana) but smaller radius
- Delay requires prediction/timing
- Punish stationary enemies or choke points
- Combo with CC to guarantee hits

## Counterplay

- Mobile units can escape during delay
- Watch for ground bubbling telegraph
