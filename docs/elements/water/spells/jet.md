# Jet

**Element:** Water
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 2
- **Damage:** 45
- **Target:** Single unit
- **Cast Type:** Fast projectile

## Effect

Cheap single-target damage. High-pressure water stream shoots at target, dealing damage on impact.

## Visual

- High-pressure water stream projectile
- Splash effect on impact
- Blue/cyan color palette
- Fast travel speed

## Tactical Use

- Water's cheap direct damage option
- Quick and precise targeting
- Poke damage and finishing blows
- Similar role to Fire's Ember but with projectile travel time
