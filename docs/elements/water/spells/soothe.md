# Soothe

**Element:** Water
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 4
- **Healing:** 40 HP
- **AOE Radius:** 8 units
- **Target:** Allied units in area

## Effect

AOE healing spell. All allied units in the target area receive burst healing.

## Visual

- Gentle rain falls in the area
- Soft blue ripples on the ground
- Healing particles rise from affected units
- Calming, restorative aesthetic

## Tactical Use

- Multi-target healing to sustain your army
- Keep units alive through sustained damage
- Less healing per unit than single-target heals, but hits everyone
- Position army together to maximize value
