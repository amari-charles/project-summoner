# Cleanse

**Element:** Water
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 2
- **AOE Radius:** 5 units
- **Target:** Allied units in area

## Effect

Remove all debuffs from allied units in the target area. Does not heal - purely removes negative status effects.

## Visual

- Wave of clear water washes over units
- Impurities/dark particles dissolve away
- Clean, purifying effect
- Crystal clear water aesthetic

## Tactical Use

- Counter to poison, slows, curses, and other debuffs
- Cheap and reactive
- Essential against debuff-heavy strategies
- Pairs with Soothe for full support coverage

## Counters

- Poison DoT effects
- Movement slows
- Attack speed debuffs
- Any negative status effect
