# Stone Ape

**Element:** Earth

## Overview

A massive gorilla made of living rock. Stone Apes are slow and deliberate, but devastating in close combat. They embody earth's patient, unstoppable nature.

## Visual Description

**Current:** Large brown box mesh (tall, wide)
**Target:** A gorilla formed from granite and moss. Hunched posture. Cracks in its stone skin glow with inner earth energy. Moves with heavy, deliberate steps.

## Gameplay Role

- **Type:** Melee
- **Rarity:** Epic
- **Archetype:** Heavy Tank/Bruiser
- Very high HP, high damage, very slow movement
- A walking wall that crushes anything in reach
