# Earth

## Gameplay Identity

**Strong, slow, enduring**

Earth summons are immovable. Their strength is in outlasting opponents—absorbing punishment and wearing enemies down through sheer endurance.

## Philosophy

Stands for stability, structure, and endurance. Earth is the foundation upon which all else is built.

## Elevated Form: Metal

Matter learns to shape itself—civilization and artifice emerge.

## Variant

- **Crystal** — Refinement, resilience, and clarity. Earth under pressure becomes something stronger.
