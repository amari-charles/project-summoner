# Fortify

**Element:** Earth
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 3
- **Damage Reduction:** 40%
- **Duration:** 4 seconds
- **AOE Radius:** TBD

## Effect

Defensive buff. Allied units in the target area gain significant damage reduction for the duration.

## Visual

- Stone texture briefly covers units
- Brown/tan glow effect
- Dust particles rising from affected units
- Units look heavier, more grounded

## Tactical Use

- Keep your army alive through burst damage
- Protect during retreat
- Hold a position against superior numbers
- Counter enemy all-in pushes
