# Quake

**Element:** Earth
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 5
- **Damage:** 60
- **AOE Radius:** 8 units
- **Stun Duration:** 1 second

## Effect

AOE damage spell with hard CC. Ground shakes violently, dealing damage to all enemies in radius and stunning them briefly.

## Visual

- Ground shakes violently
- Cracks spread outward from center
- Units stumble and stagger
- Dust and debris particles

## Tactical Use

- Less damage than Fireball but adds hard CC
- Disrupt enemy formations
- Interrupt enemy attacks and abilities
- Setup for follow-up damage
