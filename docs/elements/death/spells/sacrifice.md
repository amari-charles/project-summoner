# Sacrifice

**Element:** Death
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 0
- **Target:** Single allied unit
- **Mana Refund:** 50% of unit's original mana cost

## Effect

Resource manipulation spell. Kill one of your own units instantly and refund a portion of its mana cost.

## Visual

- Unit dissolves into dark energy
- Energy flows back to your summoner
- Mana visibly returned
- Willing sacrifice aesthetic

## Tactical Use

- Trade a unit for mana when needed
- Salvage value from nearly-dead units
- Enable bigger plays with extra mana
- Desperation resource generation

## Notes

Costs 0 mana to cast but requires sacrificing a unit. Net positive mana if the unit was expensive.
