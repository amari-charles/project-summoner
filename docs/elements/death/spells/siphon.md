# Siphon

**Element:** Death
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 4
- **Damage:** 60
- **Target:** Single enemy unit
- **Healing:** 50% of damage dealt to nearby allies
- **Heal Radius:** 5 units

## Effect

Life steal spell. Deals damage to enemy and heals all nearby allied units for half the damage dealt.

## Visual

- Life force visibly pulled from enemy as dark stream
- Dark energy splits into green wisps
- Wisps flow to nearby allies, healing them
- Vampiric drain aesthetic

## Tactical Use

- Damage + team heal in one spell
- Sustain your army while hurting theirs
- Efficient when allies are grouped
- Death's version of healing
