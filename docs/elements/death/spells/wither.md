# Wither

**Element:** Death
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 2
- **Damage:** 40
- **Target:** Single enemy unit
- **Healing Reduction:** 50% for 4 seconds

## Effect

Death's basic attack with anti-heal. Damages target and reduces healing they receive for the duration.

## Visual

- Dark energy hits target
- Sickly/decayed appearance
- Grey tint while debuffed
- Withering effect on target

## Tactical Use

- Counter to Life/Water healing
- Cheap damage with utility
- Make enemy sustain less effective
- Pressure healers and tanks
