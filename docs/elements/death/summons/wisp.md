# Wisp

**Element:** Death
**Status:** Implemented

## Overview

A common creature native to the elemental plane of death. Wisps are among the most frequently summoned creatures due to their reliability and ease of bonding with novice summoners. They exist across all elemental planes in variant forms, each shaped by their element's nature.

## Visual Description

- Teardrop-shaped body made of spectral essence
- Ghastly and hollow - edges fade into wisps of ether
- Hovers above the ground, body flickering between visible and translucent
- Pale purple/grey with darker void-like core
- No distinct features - pure elemental energy

## Gameplay Role

- **Type:** Melee
- **Archetype:** Basic attacker
- Straightforward combat unit with minimal abilities
- Embodies death's philosophy: inevitable and draining
