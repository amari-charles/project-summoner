# Wisp

**Element:** Shadow
**Status:** Implemented

## Overview

A common creature native to the elemental plane of shadow. Wisps are among the most frequently summoned creatures due to their reliability and ease of bonding with novice summoners. They exist across all elemental planes in variant forms, each shaped by their element's nature.

## Visual Description

- Teardrop-shaped body made of living shadow
- Dark and smoky - edges fade and blend with darkness
- Hovers above the ground, body shifting like smoke
- Deep black/purple with occasional darker voids within
- No distinct features - pure elemental energy

## Gameplay Role

- **Type:** Melee
- **Archetype:** Basic attacker
- Straightforward combat unit with minimal abilities
- Embodies shadow's philosophy: elusive and deceptive
