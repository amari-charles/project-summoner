# Conceal

**Element:** Shadow
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 3
- **Target:** Single allied unit

## Effect

Drop aggro spell. Target allied unit becomes untargeted - all enemies currently targeting them lose track and must re-acquire new targets.

## Visual

- Unit flickers into shadow briefly
- Enemies visibly lose track
- Confused enemy animations as they look for new targets
- Brief shadow wisps around concealed unit

## Tactical Use

- Save a focused unit from being burst down
- Make enemies waste time re-targeting
- Protect your key damage dealers or healers
- Disrupt enemy focus fire
