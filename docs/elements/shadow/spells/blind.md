# Blind

**Element:** Shadow
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 4
- **Duration:** 4 seconds
- **AOE Radius:** 6 units
- **Miss Chance:** 50%

## Effect

Debuff spell. Enemies in the area have a 50% chance to miss their attacks for the duration. Effectively halves enemy DPS.

## Visual

- Dark cloud covers enemies' vision
- Shadowy mist around their heads
- Enemies swing at empty air when they miss
- Disoriented animations

## Tactical Use

- Defensive debuff - reduces incoming damage
- Halves enemy army DPS on average
- Great against attack-speed focused enemies
- Zone control through threat reduction
