# Miasma Cloud

**Element:** Poison
**Status:** Planned

## Overview

A toxic cloud creature that exudes poisonous vapors. Miasma Clouds are corrupted relatives of Puffs, their once-pure forms now saturated with venomous gases.

## Visual Description

- Cloud creature similar in shape to Puff
- Sickly green/purple coloration
- Wisps of toxic gas constantly seeping from its form
- Occasional bubbles of poison forming and popping on its surface
- Based on Puff design (recolored with poison effects)

## Gameplay Role

- **Type:** Ranged
- **Archetype:** Damage-over-time specialist
- Shoots poison fog projectiles that linger and damage over time
- Embodies poison's philosophy: corruption and persistence
