# Toxin

**Element:** Poison
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 4
- **Poison Stacks:** 2 stacks per target
- **AOE Radius:** 7 units

## Effect

AOE poison application. Applies poison stacks to all enemies in the area. Fewer stacks per target but wide coverage.

## Visual

- Green mist cloud spreads in area
- Toxic gas lingers on affected units
- Multiple targets get poisoned look
- Area denial aesthetic

## Tactical Use

- Spread stacks across enemy army
- Slower per-unit buildup but wide coverage
- Zone denial through persistent threat
- Combo with Venom for fast threshold

## Stacking

Applies 2 stacks to each enemy. Good for spreading poison but needs follow-up to reach threshold.
