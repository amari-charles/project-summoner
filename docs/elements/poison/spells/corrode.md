# Corrode

**Element:** Poison
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 3
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Damage Amplification:** 30%
- **Target:** Single enemy unit

## Effect

Damage amplifier debuff. Target takes increased damage from all sources for the duration. Does not apply poison stacks.

## Visual

- Armor/skin visibly dissolves
- Acid dripping off target
- Pitted, corroded appearance
- Weakened look

## Tactical Use

- Makes your army and spells hit harder
- Focus fire setup on key targets
- Combo enabler - amplifies all damage
- Pairs well with stacked poison damage

## Notes

Does NOT apply poison stacks - this is a pure damage amp debuff. Use with other damage sources for maximum value.
