# Barrier

**Element:** Neutral
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 4
- **Shield Amount:** 50 damage absorbed per unit
- **AOE Radius:** TBD
- **Target:** Allied units in area

## Effect

AOE damage shield. Allied units in the area gain a shield that absorbs damage.

## Visual

- Shimmering transparent energy wraps around each unit
- Visible barrier effect
- Shield cracks as damage absorbed
- Breaks when depleted

## Tactical Use

- Generic defensive option for any army
- Absorb burst damage
- Protect during engagements
- Universal protection spell
