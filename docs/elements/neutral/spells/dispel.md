# Dispel

**Element:** Neutral
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 3
- **AOE Radius:** 6 units
- **Mode:** Choose on cast - remove enemy buffs OR remove ally debuffs

## Effect

Flexible counter spell. On cast, choose to either strip all buffs from enemies OR cleanse all debuffs from allies in the area.

## Visual

- Magic unravels visibly
- Effects dissolve into particles
- Dispelling energy wave
- Neutral/white color palette

## Tactical Use

- Counter enemy buff strategies (Blessing, Tailwind, etc.)
- Alternative cleanse option
- Flexible response to enemy plays
- Universal utility spell

## Notes

Different from Water's Cleanse - Dispel can ALSO strip enemy buffs. Choose mode when casting.
