# Mana Bolt

**Element:** Neutral
**Status:** Implemented

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 3
- **Damage:** 60
- **Cooldown:** 1.5s
- **Target:** Nearest enemy (auto-targeting)

## Effect

Fire a bolt of arcane energy at the nearest enemy unit. The spell automatically targets without requiring a click.

## Visual

- Purple glowing capsule projectile
- Trails purple particles as it flies
- Homing arc trajectory - tracks target while curving through the air
- Purple/violet color palette with emissive glow

## Tactical Use

- Basic damage spell available to all summoners
- Low cost, quick cooldown for frequent use
- Auto-targets nearest enemy for reliable hits
- Reliable neutral damage option
