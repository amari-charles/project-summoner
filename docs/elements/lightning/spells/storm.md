# Storm

**Element:** Lightning
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 6
- **Duration:** 4 seconds
- **Zone Radius:** 8 units
- **Damage:** 25 per strike
- **Strike Rate:** 3-4 strikes per second

## Effect

Zone control spell. Creates a storm that randomly strikes enemies inside for the duration. Chaotic but high total damage if enemies stay.

## Visual

- Dark clouds form above the zone
- Lightning bolts rain down randomly
- Thunder sounds
- Bright flashes with each strike

## Tactical Use

- Zone denial - enemies don't want to stay
- High total damage over duration
- Unpredictable but consistent pressure
- Force repositioning
