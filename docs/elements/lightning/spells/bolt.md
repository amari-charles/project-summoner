# Bolt

**Element:** Lightning
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 2
- **Damage:** 55
- **Target:** Single unit
- **Cast Type:** Instant (no travel time)

## Effect

Cheap high-damage single-target spell. Lightning strikes from above with no travel time - completely instant.

## Visual

- Lightning strikes from above
- Bright flash on impact
- Target jolts from electricity
- Quick and snappy effect

## Tactical Use

- Highest single-target damage of cheap spells
- Instant cast - no dodging or prediction needed
- Snipe priority targets
- Finish off low HP units
