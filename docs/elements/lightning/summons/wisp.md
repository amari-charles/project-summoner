# Wisp

**Element:** Lightning
**Status:** Implemented

## Overview

A common creature native to the elemental plane of lightning. Wisps are among the most frequently summoned creatures due to their reliability and ease of bonding with novice summoners. They exist across all elemental planes in variant forms, each shaped by their element's nature.

## Visual Description

- Teardrop-shaped body made of crackling energy
- Jagged, electric edges - constantly sparking and arcing
- Hovers above the ground, body pulsing with charge
- Bright yellow/white core with blue electrical tendrils
- No distinct features - pure elemental energy

## Gameplay Role

- **Type:** Melee
- **Archetype:** Basic attacker
- Straightforward combat unit with minimal abilities
- Embodies lightning's philosophy: swift and striking
