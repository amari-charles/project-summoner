# Fire

## Gameplay Identity

**Damage pressure, burn stacking, burst finishers**

Fire summons keep damage on enemies at all times, then convert that pressure into finishing bursts. They force mistakes through constant threat.

## In Plain Terms

Fire is the "keep hurting them until they crack" element. Even when you are not landing huge hits, Fire should still be dealing chip damage and setting up a kill.

## Ability Pattern (V1)

- Small burn effects that stack over time
- A burst button that consumes burn stacks for big damage
- Burning areas that punish clumping or standing still
- Momentum effects after takedowns (faster attacks, faster casts, or both)

## Synergy Loop

1. Apply repeated burn through basic attacks/spells
2. Force movement with flame zones and chip pressure
3. Spend burn stacks at the right moment to secure a kill
4. Use momentum from that kill to start the next takedown window

## Philosophy

Embodies vitality, passion, and transformation. Fire is not merely destruction—it is change itself.

## Elevated Form: Holy

Flame becomes sanctity. Energy with purpose, divine intention, and cleansing light.

## Variants (exploring)

- **Ash** — The aftermath of fire, decay and remnants
- **Coldfire** — Contradiction of cold and flame
- **Star** — Celestial fire, distant and radiant
