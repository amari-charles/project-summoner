# Blaze

**Element:** Fire
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 5
- **Duration:** 4-5 seconds
- **Heal per second:** TBD (allied fire units)
- **Damage per second:** TBD (all non-fire units)
- **Radius:** TBD

## Effect

Create a ring of fire at target location. Allied fire elemental units inside the ring heal over time. ALL non-fire units inside take burn damage - including your own non-fire allies.

## Visual

- Circular wall of flames on the ground
- Warm orange glow inside the ring
- Healing units get ember particles rising off them
- Burning units show damage ticks

## Tactical Use

- Rewards fire-heavy army composition
- Zone denial against enemies
- Sustain for fire units during extended fights
- Area control in key positions

## Unique

First spell with elemental affinity mechanic. Creates meaningful deckbuilding decision - mono-fire armies get full benefit, mixed armies must be careful with positioning.

## Risk/Reward

- **Full fire army:** Pure benefit - healing zone for your team, damage zone for enemies
- **Mixed army:** Must position carefully - your non-fire units will burn too
- **Against fire enemies:** They won't take damage from the burn
