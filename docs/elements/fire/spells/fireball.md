# Fireball

**Element:** Fire
**Status:** Implemented

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 5
- **Damage:** 100
- **AOE Radius:** 10 units
- **Cooldown:** 2.0s

## Effect

AOE damage spell. Projectile launches from allied base and travels to target location, dealing damage to all enemies in radius on impact.

## Visual

- Flaming projectile with fire trail
- Explosion on impact with burst of flames and sparks
- Orange/red color palette

## Tactical Use

- Bread-and-butter fire damage spell
- Punish clumped enemy formations
- Zone pressure during engagements
