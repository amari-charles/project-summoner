# Ember

**Element:** Fire
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 2
- **Damage:** 40
- **Target:** Single unit
- **Cast Type:** Instant (no travel time)

## Effect

Cheap, fast single-target damage. Instant cast with no projectile travel time - damage applies immediately on target.

## Visual

- Quick spark zaps the target
- Small flame burst on hit
- Minimal but snappy effect

## Tactical Use

- Snipe low HP units
- Finish off damaged targets
- Cheap poke damage
- Fast response to threats

## Unique

Water's "Jet" is similar but uses a projectile. Ember is instant - you see it, it's done.
