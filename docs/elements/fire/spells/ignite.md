# Ignite

**Element:** Fire
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 3
- **Mark Duration:** 4 seconds
- **Explosion Damage:** 80
- **Explosion Radius:** 6 units
- **Target:** Single unit (enemy)

## Effect

Mark a single enemy target. If the marked unit dies while the mark is active, it explodes dealing AOE damage to nearby enemies.

## Visual

- Target pulses with orange glow
- Ember particles rise from marked unit
- Big fiery explosion on death
- Clear visual indicator that unit is marked

## Tactical Use

- Cast on tanky frontline unit - when it dies, damage spreads to backline
- Punish clumped enemy formations
- Combo with burst damage to control explosion timing
- Forces enemy to spread out or risk chain damage

## Counterplay

- Kill marked unit away from your army
- Heal marked unit to delay explosion
- Spread formation to minimize explosion impact
