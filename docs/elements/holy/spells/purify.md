# Purify

**Element:** Holy
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 3
- **AOE Radius:** 6 units
- **Target:** Allied units in area

## Effect

Superior cleanse. Removes all debuffs AND all stacks (burn/frost/poison) from allies in the area. Stronger than Water's Cleanse.

## Visual

- Radiant golden wave spreads
- Dark effects dissolve into light
- Stacks visibly purged
- Divine purification aesthetic

## Tactical Use

- Hard counter to stack strategies
- Removes debuffs AND stacks
- Save allies from threshold effects
- Counter Poison/Fire/Ice builds

## Comparison to Cleanse

Water's Cleanse (2 mana) removes debuffs only. Holy's Purify (3 mana) removes debuffs AND stacks.
