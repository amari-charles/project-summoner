# Blessing

**Element:** Holy
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 4
- **Duration:** 5 seconds
- **Damage Bonus:** 30%
- **Damage Reduction:** 30%
- **Target:** Single allied unit

## Effect

Strong single-target buff. Target gains both increased damage AND increased survivability for the duration.

## Visual

- Golden aura surrounds unit
- Halo effect above head
- Divine glow throughout duration
- Blessed, empowered appearance

## Tactical Use

- Make one unit a champion
- Both offensive and defensive in one buff
- Protect and empower key units
- Strong single-target investment
