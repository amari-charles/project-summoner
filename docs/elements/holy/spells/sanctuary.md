# Sanctuary

**Element:** Holy
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 5
- **Duration:** 4 seconds
- **Zone Radius:** 6 units

## Effect

Spell immunity zone. Allied units inside the sanctuary cannot be targeted by enemy spells for the duration.

## Visual

- Golden dome of light
- Enemy spells fizzle at the boundary
- Protected interior glow
- Divine shield aesthetic

## Tactical Use

- Protect against enemy spell burst
- Zone of safety during fights
- Counter spell-heavy strategies
- Positioning matters - stay inside the dome

## Notes

Only blocks enemy SPELL targeting. Units can still be attacked by enemy units normally.
