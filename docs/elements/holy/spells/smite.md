# Smite

**Element:** Holy
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 2
- **Damage:** 50
- **Bonus Damage:** +25% vs Shadow/Death/Occultist units
- **Target:** Single enemy unit

## Effect

Holy's basic attack. Deals damage with bonus effectiveness against dark-aligned enemies.

## Visual

- Beam of golden light from above
- Radiant burst on impact
- Divine judgment aesthetic
- Bright, holy effect

## Tactical Use

- Cheap single-target damage
- Extra effective against dark elements
- Counter to Shadow/Death/Occultist strategies
- Holy's bread-and-butter damage
