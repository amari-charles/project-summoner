# Puff

**Element:** Wind
**Status:** Implemented

## Overview

A small cloud creature native to the elemental plane of wind. Puffs drift through the skies of their home plane, carried by eternal currents. They are gentle creatures that bond easily with summoners.

## Visual Description

- Small, fluffy white cloud with a rounded body
- Simple face with dot eyes
- Floats and bobs gently in the air
- Soft, cottony texture

## Gameplay Role

- **Type:** Ranged
- **Archetype:** Basic ranged attacker
- Fires wind projectiles at enemies from a distance
- Embodies wind's philosophy: elusive and hard to pin down
