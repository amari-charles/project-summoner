# Gust

**Element:** Wind
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 2
- **Damage:** 35
- **Target:** Single unit
- **Knockback:** Small (pushes target backward)

## Effect

Cheap single-target damage with utility. Sharp blast of air hits target, dealing damage and pushing them back slightly.

## Visual

- Sharp blast of air
- Target slides backward on impact
- Wind particle effects
- Green/white color palette

## Tactical Use

- Cheap damage with built-in utility
- Interrupt enemy charges
- Create space between units
- Push enemies out of position
- Disrupt melee attackers

## Unique

Combines damage and displacement in one cheap spell. The knockback is small but can interrupt key moments.
