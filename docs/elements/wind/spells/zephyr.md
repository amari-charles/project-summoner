# Zephyr

**Element:** Wind
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 3
- **Target:** Single allied unit
- **Dash Range:** 10-12 units
- **Dash Target:** Nearest enemy ranged unit in range

## Effect

Target allied unit dashes quickly toward the nearest enemy ranged unit within range. Gap closer spell that helps melee units reach backline threats.

## Visual

- Unit becomes a blur of wind
- Streaks across the field with motion trail
- Reforms at destination near target
- Green wind particles during dash

## Tactical Use

- Let melee units dive enemy backline
- Counter ranged-heavy compositions
- Close distance quickly in engagements
- Surprise flanking maneuvers

## Counterplay

- Keep ranged units protected
- Spread formation to control dash targets
- Enemy can prepare for the arrival since destination is predictable
