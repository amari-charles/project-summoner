# Tailwind

**Element:** Wind
**Status:** Planned

## Stats

- **Mana Cost:** 4
- **Duration:** 4 seconds
- **Move Speed Bonus:** +30%
- **Attack Speed Bonus:** +25%
- **AOE Radius:** TBD
- **Target:** Allied units in area

## Effect

Full offensive buff. Allied units in the target area gain increased movement speed and attack speed for the duration.

## Visual

- Green-tinted wind currents swirl around affected units
- Motion blur effect on buffed units
- Wind particles trail behind moving units
- Energetic, swift aesthetic

## Tactical Use

- All-in push engagements
- Hit harder and move faster
- Great for aggressive plays
- Engage or disengage tool
- Combo with Charge command for devastating coordinated strikes

## Unique

Combines both offensive stats (attack speed) and mobility (move speed) in one buff. Full commitment to aggression.
