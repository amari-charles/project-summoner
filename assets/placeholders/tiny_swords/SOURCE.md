# Tiny Swords Placeholder Assets

These files are **temporary prototype placeholders** for the bounded Academy
campus. They are not approved final Fateforged art.

- Source pack: [Tiny Swords](https://pixelfrog-assets.itch.io/tiny-swords)
- Author: Pixel Frog
- Pack: `Tiny Swords (Free Pack)`
- Imported: 2026-08-13
- Terms at import: usable and modifiable in personal and commercial projects;
  credit is optional; the assets may not be redistributed, resold, or repackaged
  as assets.

Only the building, character, sheep, and terrain PNGs currently used by the
prototype are stored here. The grass center is a 128x128 interior crop from
`Terrain/Tileset/Tilemap_color3.png`. Eight 64x64 crops from the atlas's first
ground patch provide its four edges and corners; the cliff faces remain unused.
Filenames and containing directories intentionally include `placeholder` so
these assets are not mistaken for project-owned production art.
